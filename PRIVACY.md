# Scrannotate privacy policy

Scrannotate is a screenshot annotation application from AppCove, Inc.

## Images and annotations

Scrannotate accesses the screen you capture or an image you choose to open.
It processes that image and your annotations on your device. The application
does not upload images, annotation text, or clipboard contents to AppCove or
run an analytics or advertising service.

The original image and editing history remain in memory while the editor is
open. Closing the editor discards that session. Scrannotate does not create
an automatic screenshot history. Images you explicitly save remain in your
chosen destination until you delete them. Images you copy remain subject to
your operating system's clipboard and clipboard-history settings.

Your operating system, clipboard tools, backups, or services such as cloud
folder synchronization may copy or synchronize saved files or clipboard
contents. Their privacy settings and policies apply separately.

## Local settings and permissions

Scrannotate stores recently used colors and chosen drawing sizes locally.
Depending on your platform, it may also remember screen-capture grants or an
authorized output folder. These settings are not sent to AppCove. You can
remove the application's local settings to reset them. Deleting settings
does not delete screenshots you saved elsewhere.

Screen-capture access is used to take the screenshot you request. You can
revoke access in your operating system's privacy settings. Opening a file or
choosing an output folder authorizes the corresponding local file operation.
The application does not require an account and has no built-in advertising,
tracking, or automatic crash-report upload.

## Support and external links

Opening Support or an online policy link launches your browser. The linked
website's privacy policy applies to that visit. Information you voluntarily
include in a support request is shared with the service hosting that request
and with the people who can view it. GitHub issues are public; do not include
private screenshots or personal information you do not wish to disclose.

For questions about Scrannotate or this policy, contact AppCove through
https://github.com/appcove/scrannotate/issues or jgarber@appcove.com.

This policy describes the application distributed with it. Changes to the
application's data practices require an accompanying policy update.
